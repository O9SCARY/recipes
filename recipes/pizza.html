Pizza Recipe


Description

This is the full recipe needed for making a pizza from the start. There are different types of pizza but this recipe is for the Magherita pizza as it is one of the esiest to make.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Flour
  • Pizza or Tomato sauce
  • Mozarella cheese

Tools

  • Gloves
  • Rolling Pin
  • Pizza Screen
  • Medium sized Ladle
  • Hot oven
  • Cutter

    • Steps

    1. Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water
    2. Put on gloves to maintain hygiene
    3. Tear a handful of dough
    4. Sprinkle some flour on the dough and use the rolling pin to roll till flat round
    5. Put the flat rounded dough on the pizza screen to fit all edges
    6. Fetch a spoonful of pizza or tomato sauce and spread it evenly around the flattened dough
    7. Flatten the edges of the dough to make it sit on the pizza screen
    8. Spread Mozarella cheese all around and put it into the hot oven
    9. After 7 to 8 minutes remove the well cooked oizza and cut into four slices
    10. Your pizza is ready to serve

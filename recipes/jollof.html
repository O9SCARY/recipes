Jollof recipe

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Blended tomatoes and pepper
  • Vegetable oil
  • Onions
  • Ginger
  • Curry powder and bay leaves
  • Chicken
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and fry until soft.
  2. Add the tomato paste and fry for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often until it darkens and the oil separates
  3. Pour in the blended tomato-pepper mixture, add curry powder, thyme, smoked paprika, and bay leaves. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until thickened.
  4. Stir in the rinsed rice so every single grain is fully coated in the red stew.
  5. Cover the pot tightly with a tight lid or foil to trap the steam. Turn the heat to low and cook for 30 to 35 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender
  6. Serve hot with chicken

Fried rice recipe

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Eggs
  • Onions
  • Carrots
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Maggi or Seasoning cubes
  • Oyster sauce
  • Salt
  • Green pepper
  • Ginger Sauce

Steps

  1. Pour some oil into the frying pan and fry two eggs
  2. Remove the eggs from the pan, pour some small oil and add ginger sauce
  3. Add portioned rice immeditely after adding the ginger sauce to the boiling pan
  4. Pour 6 teaspoonsfuls of soy sauce and mix till uniform colour
  5. Add half of the maggi or seasoning cube to the mixture and sprinkle a pinch of salt to the mixture
  6. Allow to fry and mix till uniform taste and serve warm.
